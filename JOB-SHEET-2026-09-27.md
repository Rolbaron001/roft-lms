| D1 | **Link a hand-built assessment's questions to criteria.** A question built on the platform (not captured from a paper) can name the assessment criteria it assesses; the assessor then judges it criterion by criterion, as with a captured paper; and the coverage check counts it. | Since 26 September a study unit's course cannot be published until every criterion has something assessing it. A provider who builds assessments on the platform, rather than capturing papers or uploading an alignment matrix, has no way to satisfy that except by tagging lessons. For them the check is a wall. This was W14, noted as mattering only to such providers; the coverage check made it matter. **Done, 27 September.** A question's form lists the criteria of every module the course delivers, narrowed by typing, and any number can be ticked; each question on the Assessments page shows what it assesses, changeable while the assessment is a draft. A criterion from outside the course is refused. The coverage check counts these questions ("1 question"). **The assessor's decision screen now asks about the criteria the questions assess** (before, a study unit's assessor was offered none at all, and a paper's assessor the whole module), pre-filled with what the marks propose, and asks for a reason wherever the assessor departs from it; the proposal is stored beside the decision. **Found on 121151's real data:** a code alone is ambiguous, since each module of a unit has its own IAC0104 and PM01 has one under each topic, so every criterion is now shown with its topic, "KM0101 IAC0104" and "PM0102 IAC0104". Walked on the local copy: a question built on SU1's course, linked to two criteria, one removed again, coverage shown. Six tests. | Done |
| D2 | **Record the certificate the QCTO issues.** After the EISA, the QCTO issues the qualification certificate, not the provider. The cohort archive (D3) also needs it: its rule is "archive once the cohort has its certificates", and those certificates are the QCTO's. **Done, 27 September.** A "Qualification certificate" card on the learner's readiness page, where statements are issued: staff who manage enrolments record the certificate number, the date on it and the awarding body (QCTO unless changed, since another provider's qualification can be awarded by another body). The card says plainly that the certificate comes from the awarding body, not the provider. Only for a qualification the learner is enrolled on here, once per qualification, and removed only with a reason, which the audit log keeps with what was removed. Shown on the learner's record under "Qualifications awarded". Walked on the local copy: recorded, seen on the record, removed.

We went through every one of them against your original files. Most turned out

Three things, however, are in the documents themselves. I would be grateful if
you could look at them, because only you can say what was intended.

The files are in the 121151 testing folder, under Study Unit 5.

## 1. The two Version 2 files have each other's names

| File name | What the document actually is |
|---|---|
| CA 121151 SU5 SA5 V2.docx | The **assessor guide**. Its heading reads "Assessor Guide & Marking Memorandum, Version 2". |
| CA 121151 SU5 SA5 V2 AG.docx | The **learner's script**. Its heading reads "Instructions to Learners (Version 2)". |

Version 1 is named correctly. Please confirm Version 2 is simply named the wrong
way round, so the files can be renamed. As they stand, a learner handed the file
called "V2" would receive the marking memorandum.

## 2. Version 2 asks learners to answer a Section D that is not there

The first instruction in the Version 2 learner's script says:

> Answer ALL questions in Section A, Section B, Section C, and Section D.

Version 2 has three sections: A (the case study, 65 marks), B (multiple choice,
15 marks) and C (true or false with justification, 20 marks), which make the
100 marks. The instruction appears to have been carried over from Version 1,
which does have four sections. Please confirm it should read "Section A,
Section B and Section C".

## 3. Version 1, multiple-choice question 10

> Which international standard provides guidelines for Human Resource
> Management - Quality of Hire metrics (ISO/TS 30411)?

The options are ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 30400 Series and ISO 45001, and the
assessor guide gives "ISO 30400 Series" as correct.

The question names ISO/TS 30411 itself, which is part of the ISO 30400 series,
so the answer may be given away in the question, or a learner may look for
"30411" among the options and not find it. Please decide whether the wording
should change.

A small related point in the same assessor guide: questions 10 and 14 are
numbered "10" and "14" in the answer table, where every other question is
"Q1", "Q2" and so on. It makes no difference to a reader, but it would be tidy
to make them match.

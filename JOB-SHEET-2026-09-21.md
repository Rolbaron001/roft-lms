## 4. Storage, backups and the value chain

This is the largest item and it has a deadline.

### 4.1 Backup retention
**Status:** done, 21 September. **Needs:** confirming to Heidi.

The disk reached 93 per cent. The cause was not the application data, which is
a little over 1 GB. It was 8.5 GB of backups, of which everything older than
seven days accounted for 0.08 GB.

Two fixes went in on 21 September:

* The pre deploy backup now takes the database only. The evidence archive, a
  full copy of every uploaded file at roughly 1 GB, is taken nightly instead of
  before every deploy.
* Evidence archives are kept by count, the newest three, rather than by age.
  Age released nothing because all the weight was recent.

A third fix followed the same day. Docker images were also kept by age at 72
hours, and six deploys in one day all sat inside that window, so the prune
reclaimed nothing while 4.6 GB accumulated. Images are now kept by count, the
newest two versions, as app and tools pairs.

Result: 93 per cent full down to 60 per cent, 7.5 GB free.

Heidi's own summary of the underlying problem is correct and worth keeping:
the evidence archive is the cost, not the database dump, and both halves are
required or a restored Portfolio of Evidence points at files that no longer
exist.

## 6. Tenant flexibility

**Decision:** Roland, 21 September afternoon. **Status:** building now.

The platform must fit clients who are not Curiosa: not all in South Africa,
and not all seeking accreditation. A corporate client may want a course for
employees and nothing else. A corporate client may equally be accredited to
offer a qualification, as ROFT's prospective Ranger clients would be. The
switch is therefore about what a tenant does, never about what kind of
organisation it is.

Five capability switches, each independent:

| Switch | What it governs |
|---|---|
| Accredited qualifications | Curriculum, assessment criteria, EISA, statements of results |
| Study units | The QCTO grouping between a qualification and its material |
| Programmes | Bundling deliverables into an ordered sequence |
| Statutory reporting | NLRD, WSP and ATR |
| Workplace experience | Coach sign off, workplace agreements |

Three things make this work, and only one of them is missing:

* **Terminology** already exists per tenant, with a Settings screen. Curiosa
  have set none, which is why the menu still says Courses to a provider who
  does not use the word.
* **Navigation** already exists per tenant, with an editor.
* **Feature flags** exist as a form and a database column and are **read by
  nothing**. A tenant created with learning paths switched off still sees the
  Programmes menu. That is the gap.

The structure itself does not change. Roland's working paper of 28 August,
*Qualification, Course and Programme*, checked the three tier model against
SAQA, DHET, the QCTO, Ofqual, the US CEDS standard, Moodle Workplace, Coursera
and edX. None of the seven collapses the atomic teaching unit into the bundle
above it, and four publish gates in the code depend on Course and Programme
staying separate. What changes is what each tenant sees and what each tenant
calls it.

Curiosa's configuration becomes one instance of that: qualification and
programme, study units, everything else hidden.

### 6.1 Retire "Learning paths"
The 28 August paper recommended standardising on Programme and retiring
learning path to a first use synonym at most. The interface is consistent
already. The tenant creation form still offers a checkbox labelled **Learning
paths**, which is the one place a new tenant meets the retired word.

## Why you are receiving this

On the "Check and make live" page for the Advanced Occupational Certificate:
Human Resource Management Officer (SAQA 121151), the platform lists 22 items
holding the qualification back. You asked for a list of what to correct, and
where each document is.

Every one of the 22 has been checked against the files you uploaded. Most of
them are not in your documents at all:

| What the 22 items turned out to be | How many | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| The platform misreading documents that are correct | 18 | ROFT. Nothing is needed from you. |
| Something in the documents themselves | 4 | You, below. |

The 18 come from your assessor guides labelling things differently from the
question papers: "Task 1 Model Answer" in the guide where the paper has
"Question 1 of Section 2", marking rubrics set out as "D1 / A2.1" table rows,
and "Statement 6 [NEW]". Every one of those guides does contain the marking
guidance; the platform failed to match it. In SU1's summative, the platform also
counted the numbered parts of each Section C question as extra questions, which
is why it reported 78 marks where your paper correctly says 70. These are being
fixed on our side.

All the files named below are in the qualification's documents on the platform
(Qualifications, then 121151, then Documents), under the same names as your own
copies.

## 1. SU5 SA5 Version 2: the two files have each other's names

This was raised on 27 September and is still the case in the files on the
platform.

| File name | What the document actually is |
|---|---|
| CA 121151 SU5 SA5 V2.docx | The **assessor guide**. It opens with "Section 1: Assessor Guidance & Assessment Principles (Version 2)". |
| CA 121151 SU5 SA5 V2 AG.docx | The **question paper**. It opens with the learner's instructions and Section A, the case study. |

**To correct:** swap the two names and upload both again. As they stand, a
learner handed "V2" would receive the marking memorandum.

## 2. SU5 SA5 Version 2: an instruction mentions a Section D that is not there

Also raised on 27 September and still in the paper. The first instruction to
learners reads:

> Answer ALL questions in Section A, Section B, Section C, and Section D.

Version 2 has three sections, A, B and C, which make the 100 marks.

**To correct:** "Answer ALL questions in Section A, Section B and Section C",
unless a Section D was intended.

## 3. SU5 WB1: Part D's marks do not add up

In **CA 121151 SU5 WB1 AG.docx**, Part D is headed "Short Answer Questions
Memorandum (20 Marks)". Its five questions carry 4, 6, 4, 6 and 4 marks, which
come to **24**. The workbook itself, **CA 121151 SU5 WB1 .docx**, gives the same
five values.

**To correct:** either the heading should say 24 marks, or some questions
should carry fewer marks. Only you can say which was intended; please also
check the workbook's total once Part D is settled.

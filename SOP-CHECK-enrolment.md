# The enrolment procedure, checked against the platform

**16 September 2026.** Curiosa's *Learner Enrolment Process* read line by line
against what the platform actually does, without Heidi, so that the problem
areas are known before she is asked about them. Task 5 in
TASKS-FROM-MEETINGS.md.

The procedure has two halves — full and part qualifications, then skills
programmes — which are identical except for the QCTO address and one extra step
at the end. They are checked once and the difference noted where it falls.

---

## Step by step

**"This process kicks in once the client has been invoiced and proof of payment
has been received."** — **Built.** Both dates are recorded against the cohort,
deliberately as two dates rather than one tick: the gap between them is what a
coordinator chases. A learner paying their own way is satisfied by their own
proof of payment document instead, which Roland confirmed on 15 September is
equally valid. Reported, never enforced — a coordinator may have good reason to
enrol somebody while a payment clears.

**"Create an enrolment form for the specific client (to be completed per
learner)."** — **Built.** On the platform, and also produced as a printable
document so it can be handed to a monitor or completed on paper.

**"Send form to each learner (if email addresses were provided) or to central
contact for distribution."** — **Blocked, not missing.** The platform can
address it; outbound mail refuses the login with a 535 and is waiting on Linda.
Until then the form is shared by link or printed. This is the only step of the
procedure that no work here can finish.

**"Request certified copy of ID (current certification date, not expired) —
multiple certification dates on an ID is unacceptable, illegible IDs not
acceptable."** — **Built, and tightened today.**

The certification date is captured and checked: a copy certified longer ago
than the validity window is refused at the point of upload rather than at the
point somebody assembles a statutory return, which is months later and too
late. The other two grounds were possible but unwritten — a reviewer could
refuse with any reason they typed. They are now offered by name on the QA
form, in the procedure's own words, because a rule that lives only in a Word
document gets applied differently by each person who remembers it differently.
It also makes "how often are we sending copies back, and why" answerable.

**"Certified copy of highest qualification."** — **Built**, same handling.

**"Copy of current CV."** — **Built.** Not a certified kind, so it does not
expire.

**"Receive forms and save per learner."** — **Built.** Held against the person
rather than against one enrolment, which matters: a certified copy supplied for
one programme is the same copy for the next, and making somebody supply it
twice is the sort of thing that makes a provider keep using a shared drive.

**"QA forms and attachments."** — **Built.** Accept or refuse, with a reason
required on refusal — a refusal with no reason sends a learner back with
nothing to act on.

**"Engage with learners where necessary."** — **Outside the platform, and
should stay there.** This is a phone call. Nothing is missing.

**"Enter details in LMS and Tracker."** — **Built, and worth a question.** Both
exist and both are filled. The procedure asks for the same details to be
entered twice, in two places, which is exactly the duplication a system of
record is supposed to remove. That is a conversation with Curiosa rather than a
change here — see below.

**"Complete LEISA."** — **Built.** All forty-three columns, with the leading
zeros the QCTO's data-loading specification requires kept intact.

**"Submit LEISA to learnerenrolments@qcto.org.za and ask for
acknowledgement."** — **Partly blocked.** The address is right, and the
skills-programme address (splearnerenrolments@) is right for that half.
Submitting depends on the same mail relay. "Ask for acknowledgement" is not
tracked as a state — see below.

**"Submit two FISA instruments and related documentation to the QCTO for
approval"** *(skills programmes only)* — **Built.** Two is the expected number
and the platform holds it as such.

---

## What the check turned up

**One thing fixed today.** The two named grounds for turning away an ID are now
on the screen where the check happens, rather than only in the procedure.

**One thing to raise with Curiosa: entering details twice.** The procedure says
"Enter details in LMS and Tracker". Both are supported, so nothing is broken —
but this is the duplication a system of record exists to end, and it is worth
asking whether the Tracker should be fed from the platform rather than typed
into separately. That is their process to change, not ours, and it is the kind
of thing worth settling before a monitoring visit rather than after.

**One gap worth building, and not yet built: the acknowledgement.** The
procedure says to submit the LEISA *and ask for acknowledgement*. The platform
records the submission but has no state for "acknowledged", so a submission
that was never acknowledged looks exactly like one that was. That is the whole
reason the procedure asks for it. Small, and it needs the mail relay to be
useful, so it waits with everything else behind Linda — but it is a real gap
rather than a matter of opinion, and it is written down here so it is not
mistaken for done.

**Nothing else is missing.** Every other step is either built, deliberately
outside the platform, or blocked on outbound mail.

# Demonstration: one cohort, from quote to archive

**For:** Roland Jones and Heidi Edwards  
**Prepared:** 9 October 2026  
**Where:** the development site, https://lms.roftbusiness.org

---

## Before you start

* **Use the development site, not live.** A demonstration creates a cohort,
  learners and sessions; on the development site none of it reaches Curiosa's
  real records.
* **Sign in as an administrator.** Your home page is the administrator's
  dashboard.
* **Have a few test learners ready.** Add them under People beforehand, or
  read them in from a class spreadsheet during step 4. Never use a real
  learner's name or identity number in a demonstration.
* **Have the 121151 qualification loaded.** It is already on the development
  site. If its "Check and make live" page still lists items, that is fine for
  a demonstration: step 2 shows what the page does with them.
* **How long:** about 30 minutes for the whole walk; each step stands alone,
  so a shorter demonstration can pick steps 1, 3, 6, 8 and 12.

Every screen named below is reached from the menu at the top or from a link on
the page before it. Every item on a dashboard opens the thing it names.

---

## 1. The administrator's dashboard (2 minutes)

**Home.** Point out:

* **Needs you today** across the top: learners to notify on the LEISA,
  submissions to assess, class sessions with no register, logbooks with
  coaches. Click one: it opens the item itself.
* **Cohorts running now**, each with its learners, attendance, week, next
  session and anything still to do.
* **Due in the next fortnight** down the side, with the provider's own LEISA
  target beside the QCTO's limit.

> Everything on this page is a link to where the work is done. Nothing here is
> typed; it is read from the record.

## 2. The qualification (3 minutes)

**Learning, then Qualifications**, then 121151.

* Show the structure the platform read from the documents: study units,
  modules, criteria.
* Open **Check and make live**. It lists anything that would stop a learner
  being assessed fairly, each with a link to the document or paper concerned.
* **Build again** reads the papers afresh after a corrected document is
  uploaded.

> The platform builds what the documents settle, keeps it in draft, and asks
> for one sign-off. Nothing reaches a learner until a person makes it live.

## 3. Setting up the cohort (3 minutes)

**People, then Cohorts, then New cohort.**

* Give it a name, the qualification, and the start and end dates. Dates are
  typed in the provider's own order (Settings, Dates).
* Choose how work is released: on a schedule, or everything open. Choosing
  everything open for a credit-bearing qualification brings up a reminder that
  self-study is normally for programmes without credits.

Create it. The **step bar** at the top now shows the seven steps: Set up,
Payment, Learners, Dates, Class sessions, Running it, Archive, with what to do
next.

## 4. Payment and learners (3 minutes)

* **Invoicing and payment:** record the invoice, then **Record it** when the
  payment arrives. The enrolment procedure begins here.
* **Learners:** add them one at a time, or read in a class spreadsheet.
* Open one learner's **enrolment form**. Most of it is already known; the
  learner completes the rest from their own sign-in.

## 5. The LEISA (2 minutes)

On the cohort page, **LEISA**:

* What each learner still lacks for the QCTO's LEISA workbook, each name
  opening their enrolment form.
* The deadline counted from the induction, with the provider's own target
  (Settings, LEISA target) above the QCTO's limit.
* **Draft the LEISA** for everyone not yet notified; the workbook is then
  downloaded from Enrolment notification and recorded as sent.

## 6. Dates and class sessions (4 minutes)

* **Dates:** open the planner. **Plan it for me** drafts every opening and due
  date from the qualification; adjust any of them by hand.
* **Class sessions:** **Read the roll-out schedule** takes Curiosa's roll-out
  spreadsheet, or add sessions one at a time. Each session names its study
  unit, the workbook handed out and handed in, and the meeting link.

> A workbook handed out at a session opens on that session's date. Nobody
> dates it twice.

## 7. Running a class (4 minutes)

Open a class session from the cohort page.

* **The register:** mark it, or **Read the attendance export from the
  meeting**: the Google Meet file fills in who was there, with their time in
  the call, for checking before saving.
* **Facilitation plan:** write the agenda, resources and proceedings, then
  **Open the plan**. Everything else on it (modules, workbooks, attendance,
  apologies, next lecture) comes from the record.
* Back on the cohort page, **What the cohort can receive today** shows what
  is open; **Release now** opens an item early.

## 8. Every role sees its own dashboard (5 minutes)

**People.** The **See the platform as …** strip has one button per role. Show
two or three:

* **Learner:** what to do now, what is open and when it is due, the next
  class with its meeting link, results and feedback, their badges.
* **Facilitator:** today's sessions, registers not taken, workbooks waiting,
  learners to follow up.
* **Assessor:** submissions waiting, oldest first, each opening the marking
  screen.
* **Workplace coach:** logbooks waiting for their signature.

**Stop viewing** at the top returns to your own view. Nothing can be changed
while looking.

## 9. Assessment and moderation (3 minutes)

* **Assessment, then To assess:** an assessor opens a submission and judges
  each criterion.
* **To moderate:** the moderator's sample, with the assessor's judgement on
  every criterion.
* On the cohort page, **Results** shows the cohort's grid; the cohort file's
  **Assessor Reports** gives the report per study unit, produced from the
  record.

## 10. Feedback, work experience and progress (2 minutes)

* **Ask for feedback** after a summative; learners have 48 hours. The
  questions and the scale are the provider's own (Settings, Programme
  feedback).
* **Work experience** on the cohort page: each learner against each workplace
  module, with the logbook's stage and hours.

## 11. The cohort file (2 minutes)

**Cohort file** at the top of the cohort page: the twelve folders of
Curiosa's own cohort file, each showing what is held and what is missing,
with links to where each thing is worked on.

**Download the cohort file** gives a zip in the same folders, for a monitor
or an auditor.

## 12. External assessment, certificates and archive (2 minutes)

* **Assessment, then EISA readiness:** each learner's eligibility.
* When the QCTO's certificate arrives, **Record the certificate** on the
  learner's readiness page.
* **Archive evidence**, at the top of the cohort page: the cohort's evidence
  written to one file that opens in a
  browser without the platform, checked against the provider's own copy
  before anything leaves the server.

> From the quote to the archive, every step is on screen, in order, with what
> comes next.

---

## If something goes wrong

* **A page refuses you:** you may be viewing as someone else. Press **Stop
  viewing** at the top.
* **A dashboard section is empty:** it is only as full as the record behind
  it. A coach with no learners placed has no logbooks to show.
* **Anything else:** carry on with the next step; each stands alone.
